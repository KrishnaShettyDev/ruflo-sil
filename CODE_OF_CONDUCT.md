# Code of Conduct

This project is open to anyone who wants to do good work on it. The bar is straightforward.

## What we expect

Be respectful. Engineering criticism is welcome, encouraged, and how the work gets better. Personal attacks are not.

The work is the work. Reviewers will criticize code, designs, benchmarks, and methods. They are not criticizing you. Don't take it personally and don't make it personal.

Research has disagreements. Methodological disputes are healthy. If two people read the same paper and reach different conclusions, that's interesting. If two people argue about who is smarter, that's noise.

If you're new, ask questions. If you're experienced, answer them well. Neither role is permanent.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, slurs, demeaning comments, or sustained personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Sexualized comments or imagery in any project space.
- Brigading, threats, or coordinated bad-faith pile-ons.
- Bad-faith argumentation: misrepresenting what someone said, repeatedly ignoring counter-evidence, or wasting reviewers' time with sealioning.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: GitHub issues, PRs, Discussions, any chat we set up, and direct interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can remove anyone whose behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive for others. The remedies, in order of severity, are: a private warning, a temporary block, or a permanent ban.

Report violations by emailing the maintainer at `contact@plutaslab.com`. Reports are handled privately. Retaliation against reporters is itself a violation.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), trimmed down for a small research project.
